Brand guidelines

The Home for Everyone brand.

Luxury boutique, warmly welcoming. Deep forest green for trust, brushed gold for aspiration — a brand that says premium without saying exclusive.

The logo

The key to home.

A vintage key whose bow forms a house — the promise that this brokerage hands you the keys. Always reproduce the logo from the master artwork file. Never redraw, recolor, or rearrange it.

Clear space & minimum size

Keep clear space around the logo equal to the height of the key on all sides. Minimum digital width: 120px. Minimum print width: 1 inch.

✓ Do

Use the full-color logo on white, ivory, or light backgrounds. Use the badge alone as a social avatar or favicon.

✕ Don’t

Don’t stretch, rotate, recolor, add shadows, or place the logo on busy photography without a scrim.

Buy, sell, and invest with a boutique brokerage that treats your move like its own.

Inter (fallback: system sans) — body copy, forms, navigation.

Voice

Warm. Direct. Never stuffy.

✓ We say

“Your home, launched — not just listed.”
“Tell us what you’re after. We listen first.”
“A home for everyone. Right here in Boston.”

✕ We never say

Jargon without explanation. Guarantees about prices or timelines. Anything that makes a first-time buyer feel small.
